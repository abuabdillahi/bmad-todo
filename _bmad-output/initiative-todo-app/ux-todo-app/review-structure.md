**Purpose read:** These two documents give human reviewers and agent builders a contract for building the BMad Todos UI. DESIGN.md is the visual contract (tokens, contrast, component appearance). EXPERIENCE.md is the behaviour contract (wording, states, focus, motion, flows) and also carries the hand-offs to architecture and the ticket.

**Structure model:** Reference/Database. Builders look up a component or state by name and expect one source per fact and the same layout for every entry. Key Flows is the only narrative part and is judged as Tutorial/Guide. The word counts below come from word_metrics.py: DESIGN.md has 6,531 words (820 of them frontmatter) and EXPERIENCE.md has 9,194, for 15,725 in total.

**Summary:** 29 change recommendations (CUT, MERGE, MOVE or CONDENSE) and 2 PRESERVE. If all are accepted, the estimated net reduction is about 890 words, about 5.7% of 15,725 (about 6% of the 14,905 body words without frontmatter). Most of it comes from giving each fact one home: focus, the Announcer, motion and list-state conditions. Several rows change only the form (oversized cells to tables or blocks) and leave length about the same. No length target was given. Trade-off: replacing restatements with pointers means a builder working on one component has to follow cross-references to the Focus table, Motion or the List states table. Explicit "see X › Y" anchors keep that cost low for both humans and agents. No recommendation changes a decision, a value, approved wording, the locked DESIGN section order or the YAML token tree.
