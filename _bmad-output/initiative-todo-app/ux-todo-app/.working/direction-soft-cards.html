Soft Cards

Friendly and tactile: every todo is a rounded card with generous padding, round check circles and soft-tinted buttons that are easy to hit with a thumb. On a phone the Edit and Delete buttons sit on their own line under the text, so long descriptions never get squeezed; on desktop they fold back into one line. Completed cards go hollow (dashed outline, no fill) and carry a Done chip, and failures show as a rounded banner at the very top, above the add form.

Completed todos: Card loses its fill and shadow and gets a dashed outline, a filled check circle, muted text, plus a visible 'Done' chip (shape + word, not colour). Announcer: banner at the top, under the heading and above the add form. Hex values, contrast ratios, radius, spacing and type sizes are in the <style> comment at the top of this file. Frames marked “focus ring shown” render the keyboard focus style statically so you can judge it.
