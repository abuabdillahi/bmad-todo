Visual direction 4 of 4 · Todo list screen

Bold Block

Confident and high-energy: a heavy display heading and a dark navy panel make adding a todo the hero of the screen, with a big yellow Add button. Active rows are solid blocks of bold text; when completed they recede: the block empties to an outline, the text drops from bold to regular and one size smaller, and the check fills. Failures surface in a floating dock pinned to the bottom of the screen, always in view and within thumb reach without pushing the list around.

Completed todos: 'Recede': filled row block becomes an empty outline, text weight drops 650 to 400 and one size smaller, muted text (7.4:1 light / 9.2:1 dark), solid filled check square. Shape and weight carry it, not colour; the subtlest of the four, judge it at a glance. Announcer: floating dock pinned to the bottom of the viewport. Hex values, contrast ratios, radius, spacing and type sizes are in the <style> comment at the top of this file. Frames marked “focus ring shown” render the keyboard focus style statically so you can judge it.
